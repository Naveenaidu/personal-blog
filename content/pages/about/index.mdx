Heyo, I'm Naveen. I am a "Confused Hooman" and a Software Engineer by
profession. I currently work as a Senior Software Engineer at
[Ceph](https://ceph.io/) (IBM) on the RADOS team. When I'm away from the
keyboard, I either go on treks, dabble in pottery and when all else fails I lay
my head on my dog (free therapy sessions FTW) ^^

[RADOS](https://docs.ceph.com/en/latest/rados/) (Reliable Autonomic Distributed
Object Store) is the backbone of Ceph - the distributed storage system that
provides the foundation for object, block, and file storage. It handles data
distribution, replication, and recovery across the cluster. I'm currently
working on [BlueStore](https://docs.ceph.com/en/latest/rados/configuration/bluestore-config-ref/),
Ceph's default storage backend that manages data directly on raw block devices,
bypassing the local filesystem entirely.

Previously, I spent four years at [Hasura](https://hasura.io/), where I was
designing, building and maintaining various parts of the Hasura product since
its inception back in 2020. The journey was rewarding, from supporting our first
few customers, scaling its usage many folds to creating the Hasura Data Delivery
Network.

I've been getting increasingly drawn towards distributed systems and storage
systems - there's something deeply satisfying about understanding how data is
stored, replicated, and recovered at scale.
